# Landing page photos

Drop real photos in this folder (or directly in `public/images/`) using the base names below. Accepted formats: `.webp`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` (checked in that order). No code changes needed. Until a file exists, the slot shows a branded gradient fallback with a line icon.

| Base name | Recommended size | What the photo should show |
|---|---|---|
| `hero` | 1600x1000 | Filipino college students collaborating around a laptop in a bright campus library; natural light, shallow depth of field, warm tones. Keep the subjects away from the corners, where the floating cards sit. |
| `step-profile` | 800x800 | A student filling out an online profile on a laptop or tablet. |
| `step-skills` | 800x800 | A student studying or practising skills: books, workshop, or lab. |
| `step-opportunities` | 800x800 | A student browsing internship listings on a screen. |
| `role-student` | 800x600 | A smiling student with a backpack on campus. |
| `role-coordinator` | 800x600 | A coordinator reviewing student documents at a desk. |
| `role-employer` | 800x600 | An employer and a young intern shaking hands in an office. |
| `cta-bg` | 2000x800 | A wide campus or graduation scene. It shows at about 20% opacity behind the red gradient, so keep it simple. |

Tips: use real, candid photos of St. Cecilia's College where possible, keep files under ~300 KB (webp preferred), and get consent from anyone who appears in them.
